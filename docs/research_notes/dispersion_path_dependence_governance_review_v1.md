This document is the formal governance review for the completed Dispersion Path-Dependence research module. It records the governance outcome based on the completed scientific review, IC Discovery, panel audit, and upstream scientific design records.

Scientific hypothesis:

Dispersion Path-Dependence proposed that future cross-sectional returns may depend on the recent sequence of market disagreement, not only the current magnitude of dispersion. The scientific expectation was positive h5/h10 evidence for securities showing constructive behavior through relapse, stabilization, divergence, or normalization states, with h20 treated only as durability evidence.

Implementation:

The module implemented exactly four first-batch candidates:

- `dpath_01_relapse_resilience_after_calm`
- `dpath_02_disagreement_vol_stress_divergence`
- `dpath_03_elevated_disagreement_stabilization`
- `dpath_04_consensus_without_crowding`

The candidates preserved the Platform v2 scientific lineage, predeclared horizons, contamination risks, activation logic, and formula boundaries. No additional candidates were authorized or introduced.

Panel audit:

The audited panel set was approved with notes under `PANELS_APPROVED_WITH_NOTES`. The audit confirmed exact four-candidate panel coverage, no blocked candidate artifacts, no duplicate keys, schema consistency, scientific lineage preservation, timing-policy preservation, and no panel regeneration requirement.

IC Discovery:

IC Discovery completed with classification `IC_DISCOVERY_COMPLETE_WITH_NOTES`. The family did not produce broad positive h10/h20 evidence. `dpath_01` produced the only constructive h10 mean IC result, but its positive IC rate was below 0.50 and h20 was negative. `dpath_03` produced a constructive h5 result but failed h10. `dpath_02` and `dpath_04` were empirical mismatches.

Research review:

Research Review completed with classification `RESEARCH_REVIEW_RECOMMEND_PARK` and recommendation `PARK_MODULE`. It concluded that the module was scientifically coherent and well-disciplined, but empirically weak in its OHLCV-only first batch. It explicitly did not authorize a second refinement cycle.

Why the module reached this conclusion:

The module reached `PARK_MODULE` because the completed evidence did not support advancement, validation, or refinement. Two candidates were rejected, two were parked as weak diagnostic evidence, family-level h10/h20 evidence was negative, and the research review found no scientific basis for rescue attempts.
