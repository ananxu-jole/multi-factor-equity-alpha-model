## SECTION 5 - Permanent Institutional Lessons

Permanent lessons:

1. Event topology is plausible but was not predictive under this OHLCV-only module.
2. Clustered events did not produce durable h5/h10 cross-sectional IC evidence.
3. Less negative h1 behavior is not success when h5/h10 were frozen as primary horizons.
4. h1 diagnostic behavior must not be used to rescue a medium-horizon scientific hypothesis.
5. Event clustering appears too noisy, too rapidly absorbed, or too entangled with existing families in this formulation.
6. Alignment and fragmentation may be useful as contamination diagnostics, but not as an active family result.
7. Negative results are institutional assets when generated under frozen hypotheses and audited panels.
8. Platform v2 successfully prevented post-hoc horizon shopping, target hacking, and manual promotion.

Institutional lesson for future research:

- A future event-topology program must begin with a genuinely new scientific hypothesis. It may not reuse this parked module as a refinement shell.
