## 5. Minimum Institutional Inquiry Questions

### FIRST_CONTACT_ESSENTIAL

1. Does the institution provide access to WRDS or another relevant financial-data platform for academic research?
2. If WRDS exists, does the institution subscribe to CRSP?
3. If WRDS exists, does the institution subscribe to Compustat?
4. If WRDS exists, are CRSP-Compustat linking resources or CCM-style link products included?
5. Which user categories are eligible to request access?
6. Are graduate students in the relevant program eligible, and is faculty sponsorship required?
7. How is access requested, and who controls entitlement approval?
8. Where can official product coverage and subscription scope be confirmed?
9. If you are not the right contact, who is the appropriate institutional administrator?

### FOLLOW_UP_IF_ACCESS_EXISTS

- What platform or vendor is available?
- Is off-campus access allowed?
- Are individual accounts required?
- Are there training, approval, or advisor-sponsorship prerequisites?
- Which office owns access approval?
- Which office owns license interpretation?
- Are data downloads allowed at all, or is access limited to portal/session use?

### FOLLOW_UP_IF_PRODUCT_ENTITLEMENT_EXISTS

- Which exact subscribed products, modules, tables, files, or views are included?
- What historical coverage is included?
- Are active and delisted/inactive securities included?
- Is official product documentation available to the user?
- Are field dictionaries and table inventories available?
- Are CRSP, Compustat, and CCM/link resources separately entitled?
- Are security-history, stock-name, delisting, classification, shares, market-cap, and corporate-action resources included?

### FOLLOW_UP_REQUIRING_LICENSE_OR_LEGAL_OWNER

- Are local downloads permitted?
- Are local storage and archival copies permitted?
- May raw extracts be retained, and for how long?
- May checksums, row counts, manifests, query logs, or controlled references be retained?
- May derived PIT metadata or peer-group metadata be retained?
- May transformed research artifacts survive subscription termination?
- May public code reference table, schema, or field names?
- May aggregate diagnostics or derived metadata be published?
- What repository restrictions apply?

### FOLLOW_UP_REQUIRING_TECHNICAL_ADMINISTRATOR

- Where is the official table/file inventory?
- Where are official field dictionaries?
- How are historical coverage, product modules, and update cadence documented?
- Are effective dates, publication dates, availability dates, release dates, and revision dates defined?
- Are prior source versions preserved or overwritten?
- Are ticker reuse, link windows, delisting, exchange history, classification history, shares outstanding, and corporate actions documented?
- Is any sample schema available without sample rows, and what approval is needed for later sample review?

First contact should stay concise. Detailed PIT date-semantics and retention questions belong to follow-up stages after platform and authority are established.

## 8. Institutional Response Evidence Standard

Acceptable evidence includes:

- written confirmation from an authorized administrator;
- official institutional subscription page;
- authorized product inventory;
- official database-access portal page;
- official entitlement record or approved summary;
- official license summary from licensing/legal owner;
- official product documentation route;
- official data dictionary route;
- authorized WRDS, vendor, or support response;
- formal referral to the responsible institutional office.

Insufficient evidence includes:

- informal colleague statements;
- search-engine snippets;
- generic vendor marketing pages;
- outdated library guides without confirmation;
- screenshots without provenance;
- assumptions based on institutional prestige;
- access observed through another user;
- unauthenticated forum posts;
- public vendor descriptions not tied to the institution's subscription.

Public vendor descriptions may support a candidate-source question but cannot prove entitlement, table access, historical coverage, field availability, retention rights, reproducibility rights, or publication rights.

## 14. Decision Tree

Start: `Does the institution provide an authorized relevant financial-data platform?`

- No: next-stage status `NO_RELEVANT_PLATFORM_ACCESS`; peer-relative frontier remains blocked.
- Unknown or informal: next-stage status `RESPONSE_INSUFFICIENT`; seek authorized institutional response.
- Yes: ask whether the user category is eligible.

If user eligibility is denied:

- next-stage status `USER_NOT_ELIGIBLE`; peer-relative frontier remains blocked.

If user eligibility is unknown:

- next-stage status `PLATFORM_ACCESS_EXISTS_USER_ELIGIBILITY_UNKNOWN`; ask administrator.

If user eligibility is confirmed:

- ask whether relevant products are entitled.

If CRSP, Compustat, or CCM/link entitlement is absent:

- next-stage status records the missing product; source-family path remains blocked or must be reframed.

If product entitlement is confirmed:

- ask for official documentation availability.

If documentation is unavailable:

- next-stage status `RESPONSE_INSUFFICIENT` or product-specific blocked state; no field mapping.

If documentation is available:

- review date-semantics adequacy under controlled documentation review.

If date semantics are inadequate or unknown:

- next-stage status remains blocked for PIT construction.

If date semantics are adequate:

- ask retention, archive, checksum, derived-data, and publication questions.
