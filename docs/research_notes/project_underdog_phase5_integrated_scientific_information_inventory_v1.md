## 2. Scientific Meaning Of Information

Within Project Underdog, scientific information means evidence that reduces uncertainty about a defined research question under preserved provenance, time safety, reproducibility, contamination control, and lifecycle governance.

Scientific information is not the same as:

| Concept | Why it is insufficient |
|---|---|
| Feature count | More columns can add redundancy, leakage, or noise rather than knowledge. |
| Candidate count | Candidate proliferation can obscure family concentration and negative evidence. |
| Formula count | Formula variation does not prove a new mechanism. |
| Source availability | Available external data is not authoritative evidence until role-specific authority is established. |
| Metadata availability | Static or current-state labels may help diagnostics but cannot become PIT evidence silently. |
| Correlation structure | Low or high correlation alone does not establish mechanism, independence, or validity. |
| Performance signal | Positive IC without time safety, controls, and validation discipline may be contaminated. |
| Explanatory narrative | Plausibility is useful only when paired with falsifiable claims and negative-evidence rules. |
| Implementation artifact | Code, panels, registries, or schemas do not themselves establish scientific authority. |

Information may improve Project Underdog by supporting alpha evidence, controlling confounders, defining conditions, preserving negative evidence, explaining prior findings, or identifying missing prerequisites. The inventory therefore records role and maturity separately.

## 3. Information-Role Taxonomy

| Role | Definition | Permitted use | Prohibited use without further evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `VALIDATED_ALPHA_INFORMATION` | Evidence that has survived defined validation and contamination expectations for a bounded candidate or lineage. | Scientific reference, validation-era comparison, possible registration review. | Production or ML readiness by itself. |
| `SUPPORTED_ALPHA_INFORMATION` | Discovery, refined-discovery, or conditional evidence supporting a mechanism but not fully validated or not broadly independent. | Hypothesis prioritization and comparison. | Treating as broad family proof. |
| `CONTEXTUAL_CONTROL_INFORMATION` | Evidence used to explain, control, or separate market, stress, liquidity, volatility, or peer context. | Contamination control and interpretation. | Treating the control as alpha. |
| `CONDITIONING_INFORMATION` | State information that changes when another mechanism may be meaningful. | Predeclared context framing. | Post hoc slicing or horizon rescue. |
| `COMPARATOR_OR_BENCHMARK_INFORMATION` | Information defining a baseline such as market, sector, peer, size, or no-peer comparator. | Relative interpretation and ablation design. | Claiming incrementality merely because a benchmark exists. |
| `COMMON_IDIOSYNCRATIC_DECOMPOSITION_INFORMATION` | Information separating common peer/market behavior from security-specific behavior. | Attribution, hypothesis framing, and future controls. | Alpha approval without empirical incrementality. |
| `EXPLANATORY_ONLY_INFORMATION` | Evidence that improves interpretation but is not intended as an alpha input. | Scientific explanation and documentation. | Candidate creation or formula design. |
| `FAMILY_REFINEMENT_INFORMATION` | Evidence that narrows, clarifies, or conditions an existing family. | Existing-family refinement. | Registering as a new family without distinct mechanism. |
| `DIAGNOSTIC_INFORMATION` | Useful for exploration, sanity checks, coverage review, or static enrichment. | Diagnostics only. | PIT research input, validation input, or production input. |
| `NEGATIVE_INFORMATION` | Preserved evidence of failure, null, contamination, redundancy, or retirement. | Anti-resurrection, pruning, and learning. | Ignoring or relabeling as novelty. |
| `REJECTED_OR_RETIRED_INFORMATION` | Evidence attached to parked, rejected, or retired modules or variants. | Historical constraint and reopening standard. | Candidate resurrection under new names. |
| `HYPOTHETICAL_INFORMATION` | Conceptual information role not yet backed by authoritative evidence. | Future workstream planning. | Empirical use. |
| `MISSING_REQUIRED_INFORMATION` | Information known to be required but absent. | Bottleneck identification. | Assumption substitution. |
| `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` | Role cannot yet be assigned. | Hold and document uncertainty. | Directional conclusions. |

## 4. Evidence-Maturity Taxonomy

| Maturity | Meaning | Typical lifecycle status | Inventory consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `CONCEPTUAL` | Scientific idea exists but is not yet a defined hypothesis. | Brainstorm or framework. | May guide questions only. |
| `HYPOTHESIS_DEFINED` | Falsifiable claim exists, but no formula/panel/candidate is authorized. | Phase 5 conceptual work. | Eligible for future design only after prerequisites. |
| `DESIGN_ONLY` | A design exists without qualifying evidence. | Pre-empirical or parked design. | Not evidence of alpha. |
| `DIAGNOSTIC` | Used for exploration or consistency checks. | Metadata or source diagnostics. | Cannot support PIT conclusions. |
| `DISCOVERY_EVIDENCE` | Initial empirical support exists. | Early research. | Requires refinement and controls. |
| `REFINED_DISCOVERY_EVIDENCE` | Bounded refinement improved or clarified signal. | Pre-validation candidate-lineage. | May advance to validation design if gates pass. |
| `VALIDATED` | Candidate or lineage survived defined validation expectations. | Validation/registration-review stage. | Strongest non-production research evidence. |
| `ARCHIVED_NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE` | Failure, null, parked, or retired evidence is preserved. | Negative-evidence archive. | Constrains future work. |
| `RETIRED` | Mechanism, module, or variant is no longer active. | Parked/rejected/retired. | Reopening requires high standard. |
| `BLOCKED_PENDING_EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE` | Concept depends on authority, PIT identity, context, or reproducibility evidence that does not yet exist. | Phase 5 external-information gates. | Cannot proceed empirically. |

## 11. Common-Versus-Idiosyncratic Inventory

Common-versus-idiosyncratic information would explain whether an observed state is shared by the market, sector, industry, size group, valid peers, or the security itself.

Current status:

- Market/common-state controls exist within OHLCV evidence.
- Peer-common and economic-context-common evidence does not yet exist authoritatively.
- The selected module's leading scientific role is as a common-versus-idiosyncratic decomposition candidate.

Potential contribution:

- distinguish common recovery from security-specific repair;
- distinguish peer-wide stabilization from idiosyncratic stabilization;
- identify failure to participate in peer recovery;
- reinterpret existing repair or volatility findings.

Current limitation:

- no authoritative peer substrate exists, so this role is `HYPOTHETICAL_INFORMATION` and `BLOCKED_PENDING_EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE`.

## 12. Explanatory-Information Inventory

Explanatory information improves the meaning of evidence without becoming an alpha input. Examples include:

- external context explaining that a repair signal reflects sector-wide rebound;
- identity evidence explaining why a ticker series should not be treated as continuous;
- delisting evidence explaining survivor bias;
- negative evidence explaining why a mechanism should not be reopened;
- VoV or volatility context explaining whether a recovery was risk normalization rather than price repair.

Explanatory information is scientifically valuable because it reduces false positives and improves future design, even when it produces no new candidate.

## 13. Refinement-Information Inventory

Refinement information narrows or improves an existing family. Current likely refinement paths include:

- peer context as a refinement of hostile/stress repair;
- peer context as a refinement of volatility compression;
- participation context as a refinement of liquidity repair;
- valid peer rank as a refinement of rank-stability evidence;
- external context as a refinement of VoV contamination interpretation.

Refinement is not demotion. It prevents false family proliferation by keeping mechanisms inside the evidence lineage they actually extend.
